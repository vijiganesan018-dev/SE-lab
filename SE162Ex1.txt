Problem Statement: 
The Hospital Management System (HMS) will interface with various modules and 
hardware/software components, including:
• Reception module for patient registration and inquiries
• Doctor's console for viewing patient records, prescribing treatments, and scheduling
• Patient console (web or kiosk-based) for appointment booking, billing status, and 
medical history
• Pharmacy system for issuing prescribed medicines
• Laboratory module for sample collection, testing, and reporting
• Billing system for processing all financial transactions
• Admin dashboard for hospital staff to control the workflow and manage hospital 
logistics
• Emergency module for handling urgent admissions and critical cases
• The HMS will also maintain secure and real-time communication between departments 
and synchronize data with the hospital's centralized database.
